FolderRewind 1.8.2 was published on 2026-08-03. This records dated features; see the [official Release](https://github.com/Leafuke/FolderRewind/releases/tag/v1.8.2).

{/* truncate */}

## Changes at that time

- Plugins could create full configurations at startup/settings changes.
- Plugins could intercept restore requests.
- Mini visual/animation/interaction improvements.
- Minor fixes.

Store and MSI/MSIX (.7z) x64/ARM64 assets were offered historically. The 1.9 series uses a different download format and changes plugin/history contracts; do not copy old interfaces into new plugins.

See [installation](/docs/getting-started/installation) and [1.9 upgrade](/docs/getting-started/v1-9-upgrade) for Setup EXE, migration and compatibility.
:::
